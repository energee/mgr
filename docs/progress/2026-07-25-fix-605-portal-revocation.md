- **2026-07-25 (Portal revocation is durable).** Fixed #605: revoking a customer's portal access was silently undone on the revoked user's next page load, because `DELETE /api/customers/[id]/portal-users/[userId]` hard-deleted the only record of the link and the portal layout's service-role auto-link re-created it from an email match. Revocation now stamps a `revoked_at` tombstone (migration `00276`); the layout, the Portal Access list route and the RLS policies `customer_portal_users_customer_select`, `customer_orders_select`, `customer_order_items_select`, `change_requests_customer_insert`, `customers_customer_select` and `orders_customer_lock` all require `revoked_at IS NULL`; the auto-link only provisions a customer with no row at all and skips `is_active = false`; the invite route is the only path that clears the tombstone and restores it if a re-grant fails mid-flight.
