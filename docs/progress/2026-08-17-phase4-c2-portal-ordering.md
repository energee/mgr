- **2026-08-17 (Phase 4C2 — portal customers can place orders).** `/portal/orders/new` ships over 00290's customer INSERT policy: an order builder that caps each line against unallocated stock through `use-finished-good-availability`, extracted from `change-request-builder.tsx` so the two portal write surfaces cannot drift on what counts as available. The insert satisfies the policy by construction — `order_number` omitted (DB DEFAULT), `status` pinned to `draft`, `unit_price` NULL, fulfillment columns and `version` untouched — and was verified against the local stack under real portal-customer RLS rather than on types alone: the order lands as `ORD-2026-001` with `version=1`, the line item with `unit_price` NULL, and both an elevated status and a cross-customer `customer_id` are rejected by Postgres. Building it surfaced a defect nobody had audited: 00290 and 00291 hardened the *write* path, but the *read* path was broken — `finished_goods_with_availability` is `security_invoker` over a table gated on `inventory:read`, which the `customer` role does not hold, so a portal customer read **zero** products. The picker was therefore empty not just here but on the already-shipped change-request builder's "Add Item" list, invisibly, since it landed. Migration 00292 adds `get_portal_available_products()` — `SECURITY DEFINER`, returning only brand/format identity plus a count, never lot numbers or dates, and nothing at all to a non-portal caller. `e2e/customer-order.spec.ts`'s long-standing "NOT AUTOMATABLE" skip is finally gone; the real placement test lives in `portal-orders.spec.ts` under the `chromium-portal` OTP session (the only project that can reach the portal), with `E2E_MIN_PASSING` raised 21 → 22 in the same commit. 00292 is applied locally only — F114's `deployment` reads `pending` against #818 until it is pushed live.
