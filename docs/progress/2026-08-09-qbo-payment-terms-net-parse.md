- **2026-08-09 (QBO Bill due-date: parse "Net N" instead of concatenating every digit).** `parsePaymentTermsDays` in `sync-bill.ts` stripped all non-digit characters from a supplier's free-text `payment_terms` and parsed the result as one number. The supplier form's own placeholder example, `"2% 10 Net 30"`, concatenates to `"21030"` days — a QuickBooks Bill due date ~57 years in the future. Now matches the number after `"Net"` first, falling back to the old digit-strip behavior for a plain numeric entry. Regression test added and red-checked (fails on the un-fixed function, passes with the fix). Bug patrol finding, no existing issue found.
